About

I'm Godly K Mathews, a developer from Kottayam, Kerala, India. I'm pursuing a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering at the College of Engineering, Kallooppara, under APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University.

My work spans web applications, mobile apps, and AI. I enjoy the parts where software meets a real need: helping drivers manage deliveries, finding information in Malayalam documents, or making everyday office tasks easier.

I've worked as a developer intern at Thinkvyne Digital and a student intern at ICFOSS. As a freelance developer with Coxdo Solutions, I've delivered more than five production-ready web applications. My experience page has the details.

Things I'm interested in

  • Language and AI. Malayalam text processing, document retrieval, semantic search, and locally hosted language models.
  • Useful applications. Web and mobile software that connects interfaces, APIs, and data to solve everyday problems.
  • Open source. Learning through existing projects and contributing back. You can see my contributions here.
  • Hardware experiments. Connecting Raspberry Pi and ESP32 devices with software, including my smart agriculture project.

Away from the keyboard

I enjoy sci-fi movies, experimenting with cooking, and spending time with family and friends.
