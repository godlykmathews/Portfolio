Godly K Mathews

I'm Godly, a developer from Kottayam, Kerala. I build web and mobile applications, work with AI, and contribute to open-source software. I like making things that are useful in everyday life.

I'm studying Computer Science and Engineering at the College of Engineering, Kallooppara. Alongside college, I build applications for clients at Coxdo Solutions. I also spent a summer at ICFOSS, working on Malayalam AI and locally hosted language models.

A few things I've worked on:

  • Karyadhyaksha, a multilingual, voice-first office assistant.
  • A delivery management system that reduced paper-based processes by 80%.
  • A smart agriculture system for crop monitoring and treatment guidance.
